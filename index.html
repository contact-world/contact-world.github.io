Abstract

ContactWorld is a benchmark and systematic empirical study of vision-tactile world models for contact-rich manipulation. Across 12 contact-rich tasks, we find that representation structure, cross-modal compatibility, and long-horizon robustness are key to stable vision-tactile world modeling.
